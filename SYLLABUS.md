# Syllabus — German A0 to B1 in 48 weeks
Built for an MSc student in Sustainable International Agriculture: every week carries the same grammar spine, but the vocabulary, texts and speaking tasks come from soil science, crop production, university life, fieldwork, German officialdom and job interviews.

## The numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Weeks | 48 |
| Days | 336, seven days a week |
| Daily commitment | 60 minutes core, 90 minutes full |
| Total hours | 336 at the 60-minute setting, 504 at 90 |
| Vocabulary | 890 entries across 14 decks, every noun with article and plural |
| Grammar questions | 176, tagged by week, reused cumulatively at checkpoints |
| Checkpoints | 10, at weeks 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 48 |
| Story | 16 episodes, 3001 words of German, one every three weeks |
| Model texts | 5 fully worked, with Marathi commentary |
| मराठी | a bridge note for all 48 weeks, 301 words glossed in Marathi |

The Goethe-Institut estimates 350–650 teaching units of 45 minutes to reach B1 from zero. At 60 minutes a day this course delivers 448 units; at 90 it delivers 672. The plan is therefore realistic at the lower setting and comfortable at the higher one — provided the seven-day rhythm holds.

## The daily rhythm

| Day | Focus | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neue Struktur | Meet the week's grammar. Write the rule in your own words. No drilling yet. |
| 2 | Drill | Twenty transformations, spoken before written. First quiz attempt. |
| 3 | Hören | Listen twice, then shadow 90 seconds five times and record the fifth. |
| 4 | Fachtext | An unmodified German text in your own subject. Compounds split before translated. |
| 5 | Schreiben | Produce something a German person could actually receive. |
| 6 | Sprechen | Recorded two-minute monologue plus a role-play. |
| 7 | Wiederholung | Nothing new. Review, second quiz attempt, error log read aloud. |

Every day opens with a spaced-repetition review. Each day has three core blocks (60 minutes) and one stretch block (a further 30).

## मराठीतून, इंग्रजीतून नाही

English is the wrong middle language for German grammar. English threw away grammatical gender, case endings, verb-final clauses and the तू/तुम्ही distinction — Marathi kept all four. Every week carries a मराठीचा पूल note that maps the German structure onto the Marathi one you already own, and says plainly where the bridge does not hold. See MARATHI.md for the full map.

| Marathi | German | English |
|---|---|---|
| तीन लिंगं | der / die / das | nothing |
| कर्म (ला/स) | Akkusativ | nothing |
| षष्ठी (चा/ची/चे) | Genitiv | 's, of |
| क्रियापद शेवटी | verb-final subordinate clauses | nothing |
| बेचाळीस = बे + चाळीस | zweiundvierzig | forty-two (reversed) |
| तू / तुम्ही | du / Sie | you |
| मोठा, मोठी, मोठं | großer, große, großes | big |
| 'ने' in transitive past | haben vs. sein | have only |
| कर्मणी: पेरला जातो | wird gesät | is sown |

## Phase 1 — Foundation (weeks 1–10)

From no German to handling a day: introducing yourself, times and prices, instructions, the accusative and dative, and the past tense. Ends with a full A1 mock exam.

### Week 1 — Sounds, letters and the first hello
*Laute, Buchstaben, erste Begrüßung*

Read any German word aloud correctly, spell your name and address, introduce yourself in five sentences.

**Grammar**

- The German alphabet and how spelling is dictated (buchstabieren)
- Vowel length: doubled vowels, vowel+h, vowel+single consonant vs. double consonant
- Umlauts ä / ö / ü and the ß
- The verb sein (ich bin, du bist, er/sie ist, wir sind, ihr seid, sie/Sie sind)
- W-questions: wie, wer, woher, wo
- Numbers 0–20

**Tracks**

- Studies: Say your programme out loud: 'Ich studiere Nachhaltige Internationale Landwirtschaft im dritten Semester.'
- Daily life: Greetings by time of day, formal Sie vs. informal du, saying goodbye.
- Agriculture: Read the ten most common words on a German seed bag out loud (Weizen, Gerste, Roggen, Hafer, Mais, Raps, Saatgut, Keimfähigkeit, Aussaat, Ernte).
- Work: Spell your first name, family name and email address on the phone using the German spelling alphabet.

**Vocabulary** — 30 new words from: Everyday life, University

**By Sunday**

- I can pronounce ch, r, z, w, v, sch, sp, st, pf, ei, ie, eu, äu correctly.
- I can introduce myself with name, origin, language, city and subject.
- I can understand and give a phone number and an email address.

**Written output** — Write a six-line self-introduction. Record yourself reading it. Save both in the notes box.

**Resources** — DW Nicos Weg A1, Folge 1–4; Aussprache-Guide in this app (Marathi speaker notes)

> **मराठीचा पूल** — मराठी ट ठ ड ढ मूर्धन्य आहेत — जीभ टाळूला. जर्मन t, d, n दंत्य आहेत — जिभेचं टोक दातांच्या मागे. हा एक बदल केला तर उच्चार लगेच जर्मन वाटतो. ü = ई म्हणून ओठ गोल; ö = ए म्हणून ओठ गोल. मराठीत हे दोन आवाज नाहीत, म्हणून रोज पाच मिनिटं आरशासमोर.

> **कथा, भाग 1 — Ankunft** (आगमन) unlocks this week.

### Week 2 — Regular verbs and the shape of a sentence
*Regelmäßige Verben und der Satzbau*

Build correct statements and questions with any regular verb; verb always in position 2.

**Grammar**

- Present tense of regular verbs (-e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en)
- Stem changes in -t / -d stems (arbeiten → du arbeitest)
- Position 2 rule for the conjugated verb
- Yes/no questions by inversion
- Numbers 20–1000, prices, years

**Tracks**

- Studies: Talk about your timetable verbs: studieren, lernen, hören, schreiben, arbeiten, wohnen.
- Daily life: At the supermarket: asking prices, understanding numbers said fast.
- Agriculture: Countries and crops: 'In Indien baut man viel Zuckerrohr, Baumwolle und Hirse an.'
- Work: 'Ich komme aus Indien. Ich wohne seit … in Deutschland. Ich studiere …'

**Vocabulary** — 40 new words from: Everyday life, University

**By Sunday**

- I can conjugate any regular verb without thinking.
- I can ask and answer simple yes/no questions.
- I can understand prices and quantities up to 1000.

**Written output** — Write 10 sentences about your week, then convert each into a yes/no question.

**Resources** — DW Nicos Weg A1, Folge 5–10; VHS-Lernportal A1, Kapitel 1

> **मराठीचा पूल** — इथे मराठी अडवते. मराठी SOV — 'मी शेतात काम करतो', क्रियापद शेवटी. जर्मन मुख्य वाक्यात क्रियापद दुसऱ्या जागी — 'Ich arbeite auf dem Feld.' वेळ पुढे आणली तरी क्रियापद दुसरंच: 'Morgen arbeite ich…'. ही एकच सवय ओव्हरराइड करा; बाकी सगळीकडे मराठी तुमचा मित्र आहे.

### Week 3 — Nouns, genders and the accusative
*Nomen, Genus und der Akkusativ*

Use der/die/das and den/die/das correctly for the 150 nouns you meet most often.

**Grammar**

- Gender rules that actually pay off: -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion, -tät = die; -er, -ling, -ismus = der; -chen, -lein, -ment, -um = das
- Plural formation patterns (-e, -en, -er, -s, umlaut)
- Nominative vs. accusative: only masculine changes (der → den, ein → einen)
- kein / keine / keinen
- Capitalising every noun

**Tracks**

- Studies: Objects in the lab and lecture hall: der Hörsaal, das Labor, die Probe, der Versuch.
- Daily life: Food and household nouns with correct articles.
- Agriculture: First soil words with gender: der Boden, die Bodenprobe, das Bodenprofil, der Ton, der Schluff, der Sand.
- Work: 'Ich habe einen Bachelorabschluss in Bodenkunde.' — accusative after haben.

**Vocabulary** — 45 new words from: Everyday life, University, Soil science

**By Sunday**

- I can guess the gender of a new noun from its ending with good accuracy.
- I can say what I have and what I do not have.
- I can form the plural of common nouns.

**Written output** — List 30 nouns from your own study field with article and plural. Add them to the app deck.

**Resources** — Gender trainer in this app; DW Nicos Weg A1, Folge 11–16

> **मराठीचा पूल** — मोठा फायदा. मराठीत तीन लिंगं आहेत, इंग्रजीत शून्य. der/die/das ही नवी संकल्पना नाही — फक्त नवीन वाटप. पण लिंग भाषेनुसार बदलतं: मराठी 'माती' स्त्रीलिंग, जर्मन 'der Boden' पुल्लिंग. म्हणून नाम कधीच एकटं पाठ करू नका — 'der Boden, die Böden' असंच.

### Week 4 — Having, needing, telling the time
*Haben, Brauchen, Uhrzeit*

Handle time, quantity and shopping without switching to English.

**Grammar**

- haben — full conjugation
- es gibt + accusative
- nicht vs. kein: where nicht goes in the sentence
- Official and colloquial clock time (14:30 = halb drei)
- Quantities: ein Kilo, ein Liter, eine Packung, ein Stück

**Tracks**

- Studies: Read a Vorlesungsverzeichnis entry and say when a course starts and ends.

**Vocabulary** — 45 new words from: Everyday life

**By Sunday**

- I can tell the time both ways and understand opening hours.
- I can buy anything by weight or piece.
- I can negate correctly with nicht and kein.

**Written output** — Write your real weekly timetable in German, with times spelled out in words.

**Resources** — DW Nicos Weg A1, Folge 17–22

> **मराठीचा पूल** — मराठी बेचाळीस = बे (२) + चाळीस (४०). जर्मन zweiundvierzig = दोन-आणि-चाळीस. तोच क्रम! इंग्रजी 'forty-two' उलटं आहे. इथे मराठीतून विचार करा आणि जर्मन संख्या पहिल्याच आठवड्यात सोप्या वाटतील.

### Week 5 — Separable verbs and your daily routine
*Trennbare Verben und der Tagesablauf*  **Checkpoint 1.**

Describe a whole day, hour by hour, using separable verbs correctly.

**Grammar**

- Separable prefixes: auf-, ein-, an-, aus-, mit-, vor-, zu-, ab-, zurück-
- The prefix jumps to the end of the clause
- Inseparable prefixes: be-, ver-, er-, ent-, ge-, zer-
- Modal verbs part 1: können, müssen
- Word order with a modal: modal in position 2, infinitive at the end

**Tracks**

- Studies: 'Die Vorlesung fängt um acht an und hört um zehn auf.'
- Daily life: aufstehen, einkaufen, anrufen, mitkommen, abholen, zumachen.
- Agriculture: 'Wir fangen um sechs Uhr mit der Ernte an.' — field-day routine.
- Work: 'Ich muss noch meine Masterarbeit schreiben, aber ich kann 20 Stunden arbeiten.'

**Vocabulary** — 40 new words from: Everyday life, University

**By Sunday**

- I can narrate an entire day in order.
- I can say what I can do and what I must do.
- I can place a separable prefix correctly every time.

**Written output** — Write your real Tagesablauf, 15 sentences, at least 8 separable verbs.

**Resources** — Checkpoint 1 test in this app; DW Nicos Weg A1, Folge 23–28

> **मराठीचा पूल** — मराठी 'करू शकतो' — दोन्ही क्रियापदं शेवटी. जर्मन modal: 'Ich kann das Feld düngen' — modal दुसऱ्या जागी, मूळ क्रियापद अगदी शेवटी. शेवटचं क्रियापद मराठीसारखं वाटेल. विभक्त क्रियापदं: मराठी 'आत ये', 'बाहेर पड' — दिशादर्शक शब्द वेगळा; जर्मन 'komm herein', 'steh auf'.

**Vocabulary** — 45 new words from: Everyday life, Fieldwork & safety

### Week 7 — The dative and the prepositions that trigger it
*Dativ und Dativpräpositionen*

Stop guessing after mit, bei, zu, von, aus, nach, seit.

**Grammar**

- Dative-only prepositions: aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu, gegenüber
- Dative verbs: helfen, danken, gehören, gefallen, schmecken, passen
- Contractions: zum, zur, beim, vom, im, am

**Tracks**

- Studies: 'Ich arbeite bei Professorin Weber. Ich fahre mit dem Fahrrad zur Uni.'
- Daily life: Housing: WG-Zimmer, Anmeldung, Nebenkosten, Kaution.
- Agriculture: 'Die Probe kommt aus dem Oberboden. Wir arbeiten mit dem Schlepper.'
- Work: 'Ich helfe dem Team bei der Datenauswertung.'

**Vocabulary** — 45 new words from: Everyday life, Housing

**By Sunday**

- I can use the eight dative prepositions without hesitating.

**Written output** — Write a WG-Anfrage email: who you are, what you study, what you are looking for.

**Resources** — DW Nicos Weg A1, Folge 35–40

### Week 8 — Possessives and the past you can already use

## Phase 2 — Working German (weeks 11–24)

### Week 12 — Comparing things
*Komparativ und Superlativ*

Compare yields, prices, methods and options — the backbone of scientific talk.
